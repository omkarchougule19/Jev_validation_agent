The speed race

Two AIs check the same 25 chatbot answers, one at a time, side by side. Every call happens live when you press start.

Jev is a small model built only for decisions. GPT-OSS 120B is OpenAI's open model, running on Groq, one of the fastest AI hosts there is. The 25 answers are picked at random from our 100-case test set, a mix of good answers and deliberately flawed ones. Both get the exact same answers in the same order.
