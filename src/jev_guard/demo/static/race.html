🏁 The speed race

Two AIs race to check the same 25 chatbot answers, live. Each box fills in the moment its answer is judged.

⚡ Jev is a small model built only for decisions. 🤖 GPT-OSS 120B is OpenAI's open model on Groq, one of the fastest AI hosts there is. The 25 answers are drawn at random from our 100-case test set, a mix of good answers and deliberately flawed ones, and both racers get them in the same order.

✓right call ✗wrong call ?unsure, sent to a human Tap a filled box to see that answer.

💰 Costs are projected at list prices: Jev $0.042 per 1M input tokens (output is free, via OpenRouter); GPT-OSS 120B $0.15 per 1M input and $0.60 per 1M output tokens (Groq's paid tier, where its reasoning counts as output). The race itself runs on Groq's free tier.
